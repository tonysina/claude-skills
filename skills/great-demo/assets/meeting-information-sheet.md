## Confirmed With Customer

- [ ] Date and time

- [ ] Audience list (final)
- [ ] Total duration
- [ ] Specific Capabilities to be demonstrated (agreement reached)
- [ ] Infrastructure (network, projection, AV) confirmed available

## Confirmed Internally

- [ ] Selling Team aligned on roles and timing
- [ ] SE has the completed Demo Information Sheet
- [ ] Reach agreement on which Specific Capabilities will and will not be shown ("No surprises, thank you very much")
- [ ] Rehearsal completed (if deal value warrants)

## Pre-Meeting Confirmation Call

Per Cohan: call a day or two before. Ask three questions:

1. Have meeting objectives changed?
2. Are there new audience participants?
3. Are there new time constraints?

Asking ahead gives time to adapt. Discovering changes at the start of the meeting is much worse.
