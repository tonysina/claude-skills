# Demo Information Sheet

Capture the information needed to design and deliver a Great Demo. Hand to the SE before the demo gets built. Reach explicit agreement with the SE on which Specific Capabilities will be shown.

This is the single most commonly skipped artifact in real selling. Don't skip it.
