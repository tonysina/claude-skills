## What I deliberately did not change

- **No AI vocabulary hits at all.** Zero watch-list words across Passes 1 and 2 in 157 words. Density 0 per 100, spread 0 patterns — well inside the human range.
- **"they asked for it, they have the appetite, and if it goes badly there, it goes badly somewhere we can drive to"** reads like a rule-of-three, but it passes the load-bearing test: demand, readiness, and containable blast radius. Cut any one and you lose an argument. It stays.

- The closer is concrete and slightly deadpan. Nothing generic about it.
