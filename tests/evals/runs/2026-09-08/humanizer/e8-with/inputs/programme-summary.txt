The programme began in 2017 as a small pilot in two boroughs and it expanded to seven
boroughs over the following three years and the council increased its core funding in 2020
after the interim evaluation reported positive outcomes across all three of the measured
domains.

Staff numbers grew from four to nineteen in that period and the referral pathway was
redesigned twice and the second redesign introduced a single point of access which reduced
the average wait from eleven weeks to under four weeks and this remains the most cited
achievement of the programme in subsequent reviews.

The service now operates from three sites and it receives approximately 4000 referrals each
year and roughly two thirds of those referrals come from primary care and the remainder
arrive through schools and self-referral and the balance between these routes has been
broadly stable since 2022.
