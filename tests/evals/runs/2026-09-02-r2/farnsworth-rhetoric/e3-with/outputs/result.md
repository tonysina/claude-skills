**Withdrawn candidates** (generated, then cut by the claim check — not offered as options):

- *Antithesis*, "Not more diagrams. More alignment." — the "diagrams" pole is not a claim the input makes about itself or about alternatives; the input never characterizes competing tools. Fails the swap test on an invented pole. Withdrawn, not offered with a caveat.
- *Erotema*, "Isn't it time strategy and architecture actually aligned?" — the unanswered question presupposes the audience is currently misaligned, a status-quo characterization the input does not assert. Withdrawn.
- *Antimetabole* — considered and not attempted: no reciprocal relationship in the source to reverse (visualization and governance don't invert into each other), so the trigger never fires.

---

**Recommendation: Option 3 (Hypophora).**

Reason: it is the only treatment that keeps the outcome claim — "strategic alignment" — explicit rather than implied, while still cutting the tagline to 8 words; asking and immediately answering reads as confident rather than descriptive, which the other diction-only cuts don't get for free.
