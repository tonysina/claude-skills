## Triage

Register: tagline, so one figure or none. Length: 21 words, so the budget is 1. Free work: the line is fourteen Latinate polysyllables and ends on "capabilities," which trails off.

What the line actually claims:

- Contoso is an enterprise architecture platform.
- It helps ("enabling," a hedge) organizations line their work up with strategy.
- The means are seeing the whole enterprise in one place and governing it together.

"Comprehensive" asserts nothing checkable and goes.

## Treatments

**1. Zero figures, diction only**
> Contoso: the enterprise architecture platform built to keep the business and its strategy in step.

Keeps the category name and the hedge ("built to," not "will"). Drops both mechanisms. Reads as a descriptor more than a tagline.

**2. Isocolon, three load-bearing verbs**
> Contoso: See the whole enterprise. Govern it together. Keep it on course.

See = integrated visualization. Govern together = collaborative governance. Keep on course = strategic alignment. Each member carries a claim the others don't, so it passes the load-bearing test. Ends on a stress.

**3. Antithesis, strategy against architecture**
> Contoso: Strategy says where you're going. Architecture shows where you are.

Both poles are real. Swap them and the line is false, so the contrast holds. Visualization and alignment survive. Governance does not.

**4. Hypophora, the buyer's question answered**
> Contoso: Does your architecture follow your strategy? Contoso lets you see.

Names the question the line is really about. Hedge intact ("lets you"). Governance dropped, and the name lands twice.

**5. Epistrophe, the unconventional one**
> Contoso: See the enterprise as one. Govern it as one.

"As one" carries both "integrated" and "collaborative." But strategic alignment, the stated outcome, is gone. This fails the claim check. It is here so you can see what the figure costs.

## Recommendation: treatment 2

It is the only treatment that keeps all three claims and still ends on a stressed word.

If the tagline has to stand alone with no category line beside it, use the one-sentence form:

> Contoso: Enterprise architecture you can see in full, govern together, and keep on course.
