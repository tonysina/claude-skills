## Original

> Contoso: A comprehensive enterprise architecture platform enabling organizations to achieve strategic alignment through integrated visualization and collaborative governance capabilities.

## Triage

Register: tagline. Budget: 1 figure, or none. The line is 22 words and ends on "capabilities," a Latinate word with an unstressed final syllable. It carries 14 Latinate polysyllables and four nominalizations (alignment, visualization, governance, capabilities). Diction work alone will do most of the sharpening.

Claims the line makes, which every treatment has to keep or declare dropped:

1. Contoso is an enterprise architecture platform.
2. It helps organizations align architecture with strategy.
3. It does that by letting people see the architecture as a whole.
4. And by letting them govern it together.

"Comprehensive" is a scope claim with no support in the line. Treated as "whole."

## Treatments

**1. Zero figures (diction only)**

> Contoso: enterprise architecture the whole business can see and steer.

Nominalizations back to verbs, category kept, ends on "steer." Strategic alignment is carried only by implication in "steer." This is the honest default for a tagline: compression, no ornament.

**2. Isocolon, three members**

> Contoso: See the whole architecture. Steer it together. Stay aligned.

Trigger: three claims of equal weight that take equal shape. Load-bearing test: see = visualization, steer it together = collaborative governance, stay aligned = strategic alignment. Cut any member and a claim goes with it. The members shorten 4-3-2 words so the line closes rather than trails. Ends on a stress.

**3. Hypophora**

> Contoso: Does the architecture still match the strategy? Now the whole business can see.

Trigger: the buyer's real question, asked in their words and answered in the next clause. Ends on "see." Keeps alignment and visibility. Drops governance.

**4. Antithesis**

> Contoso: enterprise architecture that lives in the business, not on a shelf.

Trigger: both poles are real, and the swap test passes (architecture on a shelf is a real state of affairs, not a straw pole). Ends on "shelf." Drops the mechanism, both visualization and governance, and adds a characterization of the alternative that the original never makes.

## Recommendation

**Treatment 2.** It is the only line that keeps all three mechanism-and-outcome claims inside a single figure and still ends on a stressed syllable. Treatment 1 is the fallback if Contoso's house voice is flat declaratives and a triad would read as a costume.

  Antimetabole. No reciprocal relationship in the original. Strategy shapes
    architecture; the line never claims the reverse. Trigger tests don't fire.
  Epistrophe on "together." Collaboration is one of three claims, not the

  A second figure inside any treatment (for example hypophora answered with a

  "Enabling organizations to achieve" became an imperative. No outcome is

  "Comprehensive" became "whole." Same scope, same lack of support.
  Dropped: "enterprise" and "platform." Category words; they belong in the
