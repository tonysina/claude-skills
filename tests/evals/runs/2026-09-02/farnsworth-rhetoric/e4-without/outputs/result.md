# Executive Summary: Platform Consolidation Initiative

**The ask:** Approve a detailed planning phase for consolidating our four customer data platforms onto one. No engineering investment is committed until a decision gate at the end of that phase.

## The problem

Acquisitions between 2021 and 2024 left us running four separate customer data platforms. That costs roughly $2.1M a year in duplicated licensing, and it means we cannot produce a single view of the customer across business units.

## The proposal

Migrate all customer data to one platform over eighteen months. This needs dedicated engineers from three teams and close coordination with the commercial organization so that customer-facing operations keep running through each transition.

## The financial case

Licensing savings pay back the implementation cost by month twenty-six. Beyond that, faster reporting cycles and better data quality for downstream analytics are likely, though harder to quantify with precision.

## What could go wrong

Three risks are material, and each is a reason to plan carefully before we commit:

- **Billing systems.** The migration touches billing while finance is implementing a new revenue recognition process. Sequencing is critical.
- **Effort uncertainty.** Two of the four platforms lack comprehensive documentation, so effort estimates carry real uncertainty.
- **Team capacity.** The engineering teams identified for this work are already committed to the regulatory reporting programme. Whether both can run in parallel has not been fully assessed.

## What has not been decided

- **Platform choice.** No platform has been selected, and evaluation criteria have not yet been agreed with stakeholders. Preliminary indications are that two of the four platforms are technically viable targets. The other two have substantial limitations in data model extensibility and integration tooling maturity. The incumbent vendor for the largest platform has signalled willingness to discuss enterprise pricing if consolidation proceeds onto their platform. That is context, not a recommendation.
- **Customer communication.** Requirements have not been scoped. Any migration that affects customer-facing reporting will require advance notice to affected accounts, and the commercial organization has already raised concerns about the volume of change notifications sent to strategic accounts this fiscal year. Coordination with account management is a prerequisite for scheduling.

## Decision requested

Approve the detailed planning phase. That phase will resolve sequencing, capacity, effort, platform selection, and customer communication. A decision gate at its conclusion determines whether any engineering investment proceeds.
