**The one thing that might trip a detector**

"Boasts", "premier" and "world-famous facility" are promotional words, and promotional puffery is on most lists of AI signs. Here it points the other way. The puffery is unsourced local pride attached to specific named venues, which is a classic pattern in human-written Wikipedia town articles. AI puffery is usually diffuse ("a vibrant hub of leisure activity") rather than a specific unsupported claim that a trampoline centre is world-famous.
