Executive Summary: Platform Consolidation Initiative

What's costing us $2.1M annually in duplicated licensing and preventing unified customer reporting? Four separate customer data platforms, one from each acquisition between 2021 and 2024.

The proposed consolidation would migrate all customer data to a single platform over eighteen months. This requires dedicated engineering resources from three separate teams and coordination with the commercial organization to maintain customer continuity during transition.

Financial modeling shows licensing savings will offset implementation costs by month twenty-six. Additional benefits—accelerated reporting cycles and improved data quality for analytics—are harder to quantify but substantial.

This carries material risk. The migration touches billing systems during a period when finance is also implementing new revenue recognition processes. Timing is critical. Additionally, two of the four platforms lack comprehensive documentation, which introduces uncertainty into effort estimation.

We also face capacity constraints. The engineering teams identified for this initiative are concurrently committed to regulatory reporting, and we haven't yet fully assessed whether these workstreams can run in parallel.

Leadership approval is requested to proceed with a detailed planning phase, with a decision gate at the conclusion of that phase prior to any committed engineering investment.

On vendor considerations: the incumbent provider is willing to discuss enterprise pricing if consolidation proceeds onto their platform. This is not a recommendation—platform selection hasn't happened and evaluation criteria aren't set. Preliminary assessment suggests two platforms would work technically; the remaining two present substantial limitations in data model extensibility and integration tooling maturity.

One final gate: customer communication. We haven't scoped it yet. Any migration affecting customer-facing reporting requires advance notification to affected accounts. The commercial organization has already raised concerns about change notification volume this year. We need account management's sign-off before we proceed.
