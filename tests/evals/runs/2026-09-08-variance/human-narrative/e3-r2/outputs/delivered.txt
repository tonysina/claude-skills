Two things worth saying before the fixes.

**B did not fire, and that's the one your style pass probably already helped.** The gate is AI-side (three of your six emotional beats run through the body: stomach dropped, chest tightened, heat up the neck), but not one corroborator went with it. No smell imagery, no weather matching the mood, low sensory density. Embodied share is 3 of 6 beats, under the 60% rule. A gate on its own isn't a finding, so leave B alone.

**The strongest signal is A, and it's the last paragraph.** In the corpus, 77% of AI stories state their theme narratorially against 52% of published human ones — a 25-percentage-point gap, the largest cluster in the taxonomy. Your essay states it twice: *"What I learned is that saying no is rarely a confrontation"* and *"Most of the time it is just information that the other person doesn't have yet."* Then it closes the loop a third time with *"Once I understood that, everything at work got easier, and I have never gone back."*

The reason that reads as AI even after a style edit: the piece already made the point concretely, one paragraph earlier, in *"She had never wanted me to do both; she had just never been told I couldn't."* The final paragraph doesn't add the idea. It explains the idea you already dramatised, which is what over-determination looks like.
