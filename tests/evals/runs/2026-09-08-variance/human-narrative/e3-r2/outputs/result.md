Your essay states it twice: *"What I learned is that saying no is rarely a confrontation"* and *"Most of the time it is just information that the other person doesn't have yet."* Then it closes the loop a third time with *"Once I understood that, everything at work got easier, and I have never gone back."*

## Intervention 1 — A, thematic restraint

Delete the final paragraph. Do not replace it with anything.

Guardrail check: does the point survive? Yes — the Dana line carries it, and the review line (*"developed strong prioritisation judgment," which made me laugh, because all I had done was ask a question*) is a better closing beat than the moral was, because it's concrete and slightly sour.

**Cost.** You lose the explicit takeaway. If this essay has a home where readers expect a stated lesson, that's a real loss and you should overrule me. Otherwise the piece gets sharper by any standard, not just by distribution match.

## Intervention 2 — D, temporal

Open on the Thursday question and work backward. Delay the "twenty-six, eleven months" fact until after the reader has seen what it cost.

Right now the essay is a chronological march: Monday, two weeks, Thursday, following week, review, moral. That march is itself part of why the ending feels foreordained — nothing can happen except arrival. Opening at the charged moment also removes the need for the closing summary, so this reinforces intervention 1.

**Cost.** D is the most invasive intervention available and its measured gaps are the smallest in the taxonomy (all four are ≤0.34 on a 1–5 scale). It is worth it here only because the essay has a genuinely charged opening line sitting in the middle of it. If the reorder makes the piece feel arranged rather than told, revert it and keep intervention 1.

## Intervention 3 — E, reader address

One second-person claim, placed where the first "yes" happens.

Not *"you might be wondering"* or *"you'll see why in a moment"* — those are signposting and a surface pass would delete them. A claim **about** the reader, not about the structure.
