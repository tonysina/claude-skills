**3. Saxon + compression**
"Contoso: where teams see the systems and align the decisions."
***
Diction work only, no figure. Cuts Latinate junk ("comprehensive," "enterprise," "strategic," "capabilities") and builds on Saxon verbs.

***RECOMMENDATION: Treatment 2 (Antithesis)***

This is the sharpest tagline. Most enterprise software taglines are abstract walls of words—yours especially was. Antithesis cuts through that by naming what you're *not* (hiding complexity) and what you *are* (clarity). It's memorable, it's differentiated, and it survives the swap test. The contrast is real: competitors either hide their architecture complexity from you (bad for you) or they make it visible (good for you). Contoso does the latter.

Claim check: The original claimed you enable "strategic alignment through visualization." This treatment says you make complexity clear, which supports both of those—you can't align strategy if you can't see the system, and you can't see it if complexity is hidden. Hedge check: none needed, both poles are real.
