Executive Summary: Platform Consolidation Initiative

We acquired four vendors between 2021 and 2024. Now we operate four separate platforms, costing us $2.1M annually in duplicate licenses and preventing unified customer reporting across the business.

We would consolidate onto a single platform in eighteen months. This requires three engineering teams and coordination with sales to keep customers running during the transition. Licensing savings offset costs by month 26. We'd also see faster reporting and cleaner data for analytics.

The risks are material. Our billing systems cannot go dark while finance implements new revenue recognition—sequencing is critical. Two platforms lack documentation, which adds effort uncertainty. Our engineering teams are already committed to the regulatory reporting program, and we haven't fully assessed whether both initiatives can proceed in parallel.

The largest platform's vendor has offered enterprise pricing if we consolidate to their platform. This is not a recommendation; platform selection hasn't occurred and evaluation criteria are undefined. Two platforms appear technically viable; two have substantial limitations in data modeling and integration.

Customer communication requirements remain unscoped. Any customer-facing migration requires advance notice, and sales has expressed concerns about notification volume this fiscal year. We would need to coordinate timing with account management.

We request approval to plan, not to commit. A decision gate follows planning, before we assign resources.
