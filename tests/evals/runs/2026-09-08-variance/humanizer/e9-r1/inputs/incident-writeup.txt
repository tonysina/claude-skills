The alert fired at 02:14 and the on-call engineer acknowledged it within four minutes and
the initial assumption was a bad deploy because a release had gone out at 23:40 the previous
evening and the rollback was started before anyone checked the dependency graph.

The rollback did not resolve the errors and by 03:30 the error rate had climbed to 18 percent
of requests and a second engineer was paged and she found that the primary and replica connection pools had both been
exhausted by a batch job that runs on the first of the month and that job had been introduced
in March and had never coincided with a deploy window before.

The batch job was killed at 04:05 and the error rate returned to baseline within nine minutes
and the release was rolled forward again at 06:00 without incident and the connection pool
limit was raised from 40 to 120 that afternoon and a scheduling guard was added the following
week to keep the batch job out of deploy windows.
