## What the November migration taught us

The migration touched billing, provisioning, support tooling, and reporting—and each team found something missing from the runbook.

What went wrong:
- Communication: Status updates went to different channels and conflicted.
- Testing: The staging environment didn't match production.
- Rollback: We'd written the procedure but hadn't practiced it.

The team got through it. Going forward, we're working on clearer handoffs between teams, closer alignment between test and production, and regular rollback drills.
