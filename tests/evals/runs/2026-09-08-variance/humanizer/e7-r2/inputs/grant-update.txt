The pilot ran in three schools from January to June. Ninety-four pupils took part,
which is fourteen short of the number we costed for, because St Anne's withdrew in
February when their SENCO left.

Attendance held at 81 percent across the six months. That is higher than the after-school
programme we ran in 2023, though the cohorts differ enough that I would not lean on the
comparison. Moreover, the teachers reported that the pupils who attended least were the
ones the referral criteria were meant to reach.

We have not yet analysed the reading scores. The data arrives from the local authority in
September, and I would rather wait for it than publish something that showcases early
numbers and gets revised later.

I have asked Priya to draft the interim note for the trustees. It will be short.
