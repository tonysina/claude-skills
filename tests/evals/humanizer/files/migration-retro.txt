What the November migration taught us

The migration was a pivotal moment in our platform's evolution, underscoring the
importance of cross-team coordination and highlighting the value of early planning.
It's not just a technical exercise, it's a test of how we work together.

The cutover touched billing and provisioning and support tooling and reporting.
Every one of those teams found something the runbook had missed.

Key learnings:
- **Communication:** Status updates were inconsistent across channels.
- **Testing:** Staging did not match production configuration.
- **Rollback:** The documented procedure had not been rehearsed.

Despite these challenges, the team demonstrated remarkable resilience. Industry
reports indicate that most migrations of this scale encounter similar friction.
The road ahead is promising as we continue to refine our processes.
